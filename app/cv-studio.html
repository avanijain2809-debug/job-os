Welcome to CV Studio

CV Studio turns your real experience into a CV tailored to one specific job, without inventing anything. Your data stays in this browser on this computer.

How it works

  1. Build your profile once. Paste your current CV. Claude turns it into an "evidence bank": one entry per project or achievement, with what you actually did and which numbers are exact. You check it and answer a few questions.
  2. Tailor for each job. Paste a job description. Claude decides how to position you, picks your strongest relevant evidence and writes the CV. You also get the reasoning, the gaps and questions for you.
  3. Edit, check and save. Adjust the text, watch the one-page indicator and automatic checks, then print to PDF or download a Word file.

Two ways to use Claude

Copy-paste (default, no setup)

CV Studio prepares the full instructions. You copy them into your own Claude chat at claude.ai, then paste Claude's answer back here. Works with a normal Claude plan.

API key (optional, fully automatic)

Add an Anthropic API key in Settings and everything happens inside this page. Usage is billed to your API account. It can also read a job posting from a link.

About

CV Studio is part of JobOS. It works from one rule: every claim in the CV must trace to your own evidence. It can reframe and sharpen what you did, but never invents metrics, responsibilities, clients, skills or seniority.
